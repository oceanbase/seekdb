retain KV snapshot and GC protection until its owner ends, and publish the
entire main/LOB binding unit and owned entries with one commit. Remove the

identity for empty-shell conversion. Finish undecided replay transactions via
a durable abort before local transaction activation; otherwise logged CREATE
MDS remains permanently pending after a crash.
